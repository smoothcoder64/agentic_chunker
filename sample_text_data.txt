PIPELINE INTEGRITY MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. INTRODUCTION
This Pipeline Integrity Management Policy outlines the procedures and requirements for maintaining the safety and reliability of pipeline operations in accordance with 49 CFR Part 192.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all pipeline operations within high consequence areas as defined in 49 CFR 192.903.

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Integrity Management: A systematic approach to evaluating and managing pipeline risks to reduce both the likelihood and consequences of incidents.
3.2 High Consequence Area (HCA): Areas where pipeline releases could have greater consequences to health, safety, or environment.
3.3 Cathodic Protection: A technique to control corrosion of metal surfaces by making the metal work as a cathode in an electrochemical cell.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1 The Pipeline Safety Officer shall ensure compliance with all applicable regulations.
4.2 All personnel shall report any potential integrity issues immediately to their supervisor.
4.3 Contractors shall adhere to this policy when performing work on pipeline facilities.

5. INSPECTION REQUIREMENTS
5.1 All pipelines within HCAs shall be inspected using in-line inspection tools at intervals not exceeding 5 years.
5.2 Cathodic protection systems shall be tested annually in accordance with 49 CFR 192.451.
5.3 Direct assessment methods shall be employed when in-line inspection is not feasible.

6. RISK ASSESSMENT
6.1 A comprehensive risk assessment shall be performed annually for all pipeline segments.
6.2 Risk factors shall include but are not limited to corrosion, third-party damage, manufacturing defects, and environmental factors.

7. PREVENTIVE AND MITIGATIVE MEASURES
7.1 Based on risk assessment results, appropriate preventive and mitigative measures shall be implemented.
7.2 Additional measures shall be considered for HCAs, including increased patrol frequency, emergency response drills, and public awareness programs.

8. DOCUMENTATION AND RECORD KEEPING
8.1 All inspection, testing, and maintenance activities shall be documented.
8.2 Records shall be maintained for the life of the pipeline.
8.3 Reports shall be submitted to PHMSA as required by applicable regulations.

9. FORCE MAJEURE
9.1 In the event of force majeure, the company shall take all reasonable steps to continue compliance with this policy to the extent possible.
9.2 Any deviations from this policy due to force majeure shall be documented and reported to the appropriate regulatory authorities.

10. REVIEW AND REVISION
10.1 This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Pipeline Safety Committee.
10.2 Revisions shall be approved by the Chief Executive Officer.
